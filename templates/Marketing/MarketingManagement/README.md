## AI Skills

Before writing a marketing plan, researching a market, segmenting and
targeting, designing a product, service, or brand, setting prices,
planning communications, campaigns, sales, or channels, or planning
growth, loyalty, international expansion, or responsible marketing, find
the skills that apply to the task in the tables below and read them.

Do not read every skill file by default. Read only the skills whose
"Use when" matches the task, and follow their references to other skills
only when needed.

Each skill comes from one chapter of Philip Kotler, Kevin Lane Keller and
Alexander Chernev's *Marketing Management* (16th edition, Global Edition,
Pearson, 2022), grouped by the book's seven parts. Case studies are
reduced to one-line illustrations and the worked examples are new. The
book's statistics and platform examples age quickly; the skills carry a
short "Current note" where practice or regulation has moved (marketplaces,
privacy and consent, influencer disclosure, green claims).

# Context for AI: Marketing Management

## 1. Plan Top Down

Work from goal and target market, through the value proposition, to the
marketing mix (product, service, brand, price, incentives, communication,
distribution). A tactic that doesn't trace back to a target and a value
proposition is unanchored.

## 2. Create Value for Three Parties

A sound offer creates value for the target customers, the company, and its
collaborators. Check all three before recommending it.

## 3. Keep the Mix Consistent

Price, brand, channels, and communications must tell the same story about
the same position. Flag contradictions (a premium brand on deep discount,
a mass channel for an exclusive offer).

## 4. Measure

Tie each element to metrics (awareness, consideration, conversion,
retention, CLV, ROI) and say how results will be tracked.

## 5. Market Responsibly

No deceptive claims, fake reference prices, hidden fees, unconsented data
use, or unsupported green claims. Check local advertising and privacy
rules before recommending tactics.
