# Definition of Ready

A backlog item may enter Sprint Planning only when every check below holds. The PO and the Tech Lead check it together in Backlog Refinement. Edit this list to fit the project; changes come from retrospectives.

- [ ] The story says who wants what and why, in the user's terms.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable and testable, and the PO has confirmed them with the user when product intent was unclear.
- [ ] No open product question blocks the item; remaining questions are recorded with who answers them.
- [ ] The specification exists when the item needs one, and `spec-checklist` found no unresolved material gap.
- [ ] Examples or scenarios exist when the item has business rules worth illustrating.
- [ ] The Tech Lead has checked feasibility, risks, and dependencies, and named the ADRs that govern it.
- [ ] Data and infrastructure impact is known: whether the DBA or DevOps is needed.
- [ ] The item is small enough to finish within one sprint; otherwise it is split.
