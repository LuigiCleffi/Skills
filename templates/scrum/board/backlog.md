# Product Backlog

Owned by the PO. Ordered top to bottom: the first Ready item is the next one the team pulls into a sprint. Only the PO adds, reorders, accepts, or rejects items.

**Status:** New → Refined → Ready → In Sprint → Done → Accepted | Rejected

- **Ready** means the item meets `docs/scrum/definition-of-ready.md`.
- **Done** is set by the Tech Lead when every task meets the Definition of Done.
- **Accepted** or **Rejected** is set by the PO after the user's answer at the Sprint Review (G3).
